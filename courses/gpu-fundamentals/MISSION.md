# Course mission

Teach engineers to explain H100 execution, memory, precision, scheduling,
topology, sharing, and health evidence before attempting optimization.

The course uses Python and PyTorch and one full non-MIG H100 for local effects.
Its topology and collective explanations prepare learners for the distributed
experiments owned by Advanced Labs, which require a qualified two-eight-H100
cluster. Operational labs are read-only and do not reconfigure the GPU or cluster.
