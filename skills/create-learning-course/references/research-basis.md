# Research Basis And Applicability

## How These Rules Were Chosen

The user's established digital-textbook preferences determine the visual
standard: light colors, a wide reading frame, side TOC, contextual diagrams,
complete prose/source and seven-part practical guides. These are project
conventions, not claims that one layout is universally best.

The public sources below inform teaching and accessibility. The adaptations
are our synthesis, not copied source templates. Repository requirements and
direct user intent supplied the local context; private organizational chat and
document searches were not relevant to this reusable public standard.

## Instructional Alignment

[Carnegie Mellon Eberly Center: aligning objectives, assessments and instruction](https://www.cmu.edu/teaching/assessment/basics/alignment.html)
explains why these three elements must reinforce each other. Apply this by
choosing the observable outcome and evidence before the lesson outline. Match
an analytical or design outcome with analysis or design practice, not only a
recall quiz.

## Worked Examples, Retrieval And Spacing

[IES What Works Clearinghouse: Organizing Instruction and Study](https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/WWC/PracticeGuide/1)
supports spaced study, alternating worked solutions with problem solving,
connecting concrete and abstract representations, and combining explanatory
graphics with words. Its evidence ratings vary: not every recommendation has
equally strong support.

Apply these ideas through small worked examples, guided then independent
practice, delayed retrieval and explanatory questions. Adapt to learner
readiness; do not treat every added quiz or picture as beneficial.

## Accessibility And Learner Agency

[CAST Universal Design for Learning Guidelines 3.0](https://udlguidelines.cast.org/)
emphasizes vocabulary clarity, prior-knowledge connections, meaningful goals,
actionable feedback and graduated support. Apply these through beginner
primers, readiness checks, clear notation and appropriate ways to demonstrate
competence. This does not require multimedia decoration or fixed learning-style
labels.

## Reflow, Contrast And Diagrams

[W3C WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/) is the accessibility target,
not an automatic certification. Use semantic structure and assess with both
mechanical checks and human inspection.

[W3C Reflow](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/reflow.html)
describes the 320 CSS-pixel width target and limited exceptions for content
that genuinely needs two-dimensional layout. Keep such scrolling local; it
does not justify making the entire page pan horizontally.

[W3C Contrast Minimum](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html)
defines the normal/large text contrast thresholds. Check actual foreground and
background combinations rather than assuming a light theme is accessible.

[W3C Complex Images](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/images/complex/)
explains short identification and a fuller equivalent for essential visual
information. Our format uses SVG title/description plus visible contextual
prose and captions; removing a redundant text expander must not remove that
information.

## Skill Packaging

[OpenAI Build skills](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/build-skills)
describes portable skill packaging and progressive disclosure. Keep the
instruction core concise, load deeper references when needed and preserve
the target's explicit-only policy in agent metadata. Static packaging tests
do not demonstrate fresh-session discovery or output quality.

## Refresh Rule

Recheck current official guidance when a course relies on changing technology,
standards or product behavior. Record exact compatibility/test evidence in
author review records; do not put research-review datelines in learner-facing
Where to Go Next sections. No source here justifies a guaranteed learning,
performance or accessibility outcome without the relevant evaluation.

## Format And Evidence Boundary

The four lesson headings, conceptual title convention and requirement for a
core diagram in each explanation are this authoring kit's presentation
contract. Research-informed practices such as worked examples, retrieval and
integrated verbal/visual explanation support teaching choices; they do not
prove that these exact labels guarantee better learning. Review complete prose
and diagram meaning separately from structural compliance.
